MidCart – E-Commerce Business Analysis Project
📌 Project Overview

MidCart is an e-commerce business analysis project focused on analyzing customer, product, and order data to identify business insights and improve the overall order management process.

The project demonstrates practical Business Analyst and Data Analyst skills, including requirements documentation, process analysis, Excel data analysis, SQL, and business reporting.

🎯 Project Objectives
Analyze customer and order data to understand business performance.
Identify trends in sales, orders, customers, and products.
Analyze the end-to-end order management workflow.
Identify potential process gaps and improvement opportunities.
Create structured business requirements documentation.
Generate actionable insights to support business decision-making.
